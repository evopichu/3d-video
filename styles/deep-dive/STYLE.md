# Deep Dive 3D — style bible

**Purpose:** make invisible mechanisms visible. Best for body, health, science, engineering, and “what happens if?” explainers.

## Look
- Hero subject: sculpted, simplified 3D anatomy, machine, molecule, or everyday object.
- Sets: clean sunlit studio, laboratory void, macro-scale internal environment, or stylized physical cross-section.
- Materials: matte ceramic, translucent cyan tissue/glass, brushed metal, soft subsurface-like color, restrained emissive lines.
- Palette: warm ivory/peach base, cyan/teal internal systems, amber for attention, red only for genuine danger.
- Camera: macro push-ins, controlled orbit, fly-throughs, rack-focus simulation by selective blur/depth staging.
- Lighting: large soft key, cool rim, readable contact shadows; internal systems glow only when narratively justified.

## Shot anatomy
1. Establish the person/object at a readable scale.
2. Push into the mechanism.
3. Reveal a cutaway or internal pathway.
4. Follow one moving signal/particle through the system.
5. Pull back to show the consequence.

## Rules
Keep one visual mode per shot. If the subject is shown as an x-ray/cutaway, do not leave opaque clothing or surfaces blocking the reveal. Show cause before effect. Use labels sparingly; captions carry the story.
