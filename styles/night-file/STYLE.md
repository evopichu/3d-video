# Night File 3D — style bible

**Purpose:** atmospheric mystery and true-crime storytelling without sensationalizing real harm.

## Look
- Fully built 3D rooms, corridors, roads, vehicles, landscapes, and evidence tables.
- Palette: near-black navy, desaturated steel, dirty amber practicals, one controlled crimson accent.
- Characters: anonymous stylized 3D silhouettes or faceless figures; avoid implying an unverified likeness.
- Materials: aged paint, wet asphalt, paper, glass, brushed metal, dusty surfaces.
- Lighting: motivated pools of light, hard silhouettes, rim light, subtle fog/particles. Preserve readable blacks on mobile.
- Camera: slow dolly, doorway reveal, low tracking shot, top-down evidence-table orbit.

## Shot anatomy
1. Establish the location and date/time.
2. Reveal a person/object through silhouette.
3. Move through a clue or evidence prop.
4. Reconstruct the key event with spatial clarity.
5. End on the unanswered detail or consequence.

## Rules
Use careful language for allegations and unresolved cases. Separate established facts from theories. Never invent evidence, dialogue, or motives.
