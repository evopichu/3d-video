# Tall Tale 3D — style bible

**Purpose:** funny, surprising true stories and odd real-world incidents.

## Look
- A coherent miniature 3D world: streets, rooms, shops, offices, vehicles, and props.
- Characters: rounded stylized 3D people with readable poses, oversized but tasteful expressions, clear silhouettes.
- Palette: sunny sky blue, warm cream, saturated green, coral/orange accents.
- Materials: soft-touch plastic/clay, painted wood, glass, fabric-like roughness.
- Camera: energetic dolly/tracking shots, playful orbit, whip-pan only when motivated, strong foreground parallax.
- Animation: anticipation, squash-and-stretch used subtly, prop handoffs, clear reactions.

## Shot anatomy
1. Introduce the character and the absurd premise.
2. Show the first attempt.
3. Escalate with a visual complication.
4. Reveal the clever loophole/twist.
5. Land on a comic reaction or loop.

## Rules
Keep the world consistent between cuts. Make the joke understandable even with sound off, but let narration add the detail. For real stories, distinguish documented events from embellishment.
